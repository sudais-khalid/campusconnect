# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want CampusConnect to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or ability. We pledge to act in ways that keep this community open, respectful and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the students and staff who rely on it.
- Respect privacy: never share personal data from complaints, accounts or logs.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's Security tab (private report) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
